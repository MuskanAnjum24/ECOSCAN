Know Your Plastics

Different plastics have different recycling rules. Learn to identify them by their resin code.

✅ RECYCLABLE
#1 PET
Polyethylene Terephthalate

Clear, lightweight, and widely recycled. Commonly used for water and soda bottles.

Found in: Water bottles, juice containers

✅ RECYCLABLE
#2 HDPE
High-Density Polyethylene

Durable, stiff plastic accepted at most recycling centers.

Found in: Milk jugs, shampoo bottles, detergent

⚠️ SOMETIMES RECYCLED
#3 PVC
Polyvinyl Chloride

Rarely accepted in curbside recycling. Contains harmful chemicals when burned.

Found in: Pipes, window frames, blister packs

⚠️ SOMETIMES RECYCLED
#4 LDPE
Low-Density Polyethylene

Soft and flexible. Not accepted in most curbside programs — needs drop-off points.

Found in: Plastic bags, squeezable bottles, bread bags

✅ RECYCLABLE
#5 PP
Polypropylene

Heat resistant and increasingly accepted in recycling. Safe for food contact.

Found in: Yogurt cups, bottle caps, straws

❌ NOT RECYCLABLE
#6 PS
Polystyrene (Styrofoam)

Rarely recycled and highly polluting. Breaks into microplastics easily. Avoid when possible.

Found in: Disposable cups, takeaway boxes, packing foam

Did You Know?

Ocean Threat

By 2050, there could be more plastic in the ocean than fish by weight if current trends continue.

Microplastics in Water

Microplastics have been found in tap water, bottled water, and even in human blood and lungs.

Recycling Saves Energy

Recycling one plastic bottle saves enough energy to power a 60W light bulb for 6 hours.

India's Plastic Problem

India generates over 3.5 million tonnes of plastic waste annually, with less than 30% collected for recycling.

Alternatives Exist

Bamboo, glass, cotton, and paper alternatives can replace most single-use plastics in everyday life.

Production Keeps Rising

Global plastic production has doubled since 2000 and is expected to triple by 2060 without intervention.

Simple Ways to Reduce Plastic

Carry a Reusable Bag

Say no to plastic carry bags. A cloth bag can replace 500+ plastic bags over its lifetime.

Use a Water Bottle

A reusable bottle prevents ~156 plastic bottles per person per year from entering landfills.

Avoid Single-Use Cutlery

Carry your own fork and spoon. Single-use plastic cutlery is almost never recycled.

Sort Before You Throw

Separate plastic, paper, and organic waste. Proper sorting dramatically improves recycling rates.

Choose Minimal Packaging

Buy products with less packaging. Prefer glass or cardboard over multi-layer plastic wrapping.

Scan Before You Discard

Use EcoScan to check if an item is recyclable before throwing it away. Every scan helps.
